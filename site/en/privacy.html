Privacy policy

Rotor · Français

Effective November 6, 2026. This policy explains what information the game Rotor: Daily Lock ("Rotor") collects, why, and how to exercise your rights. Rotor is published by Consultation Behtoyi Inc. ("we"), La Prairie, Québec, Canada.

What we don't collect

Rotor doesn't ask for an account, name, email or phone number. Your progress (levels, stars, coins, streak) is stored on your device and may be included in your device backup (iCloud or Google). We can't access it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Rotor shows optional reward videos and, after level 35, occasional end-of-level ads. These ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising identifier, technical information (model, OS, language), approximate location inferred from your IP address, and your interactions with ads, to serve, measure and cap ads and to prevent fraud.

  • On iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier is used for tracking only if you allow it in the "Allow tracking" prompt. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Google settings.
  • Where the law requires it, a Google consent form lets you accept or refuse personalized ads.

See also: How Google uses data.

Purchases (RevenueCat, Apple, Google)

In-app purchases (remove ads, coins) are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate and restore purchases; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your Rotor purchase history.

Diagnostics

If we add usage statistics or crash reports to improve the game, they will be anonymous, won't be used for ad tracking, and this policy will be updated before they're turned on.

Transfers outside Québec

Google and RevenueCat may process information outside Québec and Canada, including in the United States. These providers are required to protect it.

Children

Rotor isn't directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Your rights and who is responsible

You can request access to or correction of your information, or withdraw consent, by writing to rotor@behtoyi.com. The person in charge of the protection of personal information is the president of Consultation Behtoyi Inc., reachable at the same address. You may also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

Changes

We'll post any change on this page with its effective date.
