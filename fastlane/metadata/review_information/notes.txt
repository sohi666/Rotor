2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Rotor: Daily Lock is a single-player logic puzzle game for teens and adults. Each level is a combination lock made of concentric rings: the player turns the rings so the coloured pins line up on every spoke. It offers short, calm brain-teaser sessions that work offline, a new Daily Lock puzzle every day whose result can be shared, and 200 levels that a solver has proven solvable. Every colour also has a symbol, so colour-blind players can play.

3. HOW TO USE IT
No setup, account, credentials or sample files are needed. Launch the app, then:
- Home > Play continues the campaign. Drag a ring to turn it; a level is solved when every spoke lines up.
- Home > Daily Lock opens today's puzzle.
- Coins are earned by playing and spent on hints, undos and cosmetic dial styles (Home > Lockbox). Stars fill star chests (one every 15 stars) and a 7-day key calendar gives a small daily reward. Contents are always shown before they are given.
- Bonus rounds appear after the first clear of level 5 (then 15, 25...: "Safecracker") and level 10 (every 10th level: "Golden Rush"). They can be skipped and cannot be lost. Playing levels 1 to 10 shows both.
- Settings: language (French/English), sound, vibration, colour symbols, privacy policy and terms.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
The game runs entirely on the device and needs no network connection. The only network use is:
- Expo EAS Update (expo.dev): at launch the app checks for bug-fix updates to its own game code. No personal data is sent.
- The privacy policy, terms and support pages (GitHub Pages) open in the browser when the player taps those links.
- Sharing a Daily Lock result uses the standard iOS share sheet, only when the player taps Share.
No account, analytics, advertising, AI or payment service is used. The RevenueCat purchase library is in the binary for a future version but is never started in 1.0: there are no in-app purchases.

5. REGIONS
The app works the same in every region, with the same features and content. The interface follows the device language: English or French.
