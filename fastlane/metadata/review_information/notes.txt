No account or login is required, and the game works fully offline.

This version shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases. Coins are earned by playing and spent on hints, undos and cosmetic dial styles.

Bonus rounds: after the first clear of level 5 (then 15, 25...) a "Safecracker" round opens; after level 10 (every 10th level) a "Golden Rush" round opens. Both have a Skip button and can't be lost. To see them quickly, play levels 1 to 5.

Aucun compte n'est requis et le jeu fonctionne entièrement hors ligne. Cette version n'affiche aucune publicité et ne propose aucun achat intégré.
