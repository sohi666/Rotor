No account or login is required, and the game works offline.

Ads are served by Google AdMob: rewarded videos are optional, and occasional end-of-level ads start after level 35. Permission to track is requested through App Tracking Transparency.

The non-consumable in-app purchase "Remove ads" turns off the end-of-level ads.
